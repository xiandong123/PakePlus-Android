Full Surge Pricing Policy

1. Real-Time Supply & Demand Surge (Core Rule)

Fewer available vehicles means higher surge multiplier, capped at 1.5x. 100% of surge revenue belongs to drivers, platform takes no commission on surcharge.

≥4 vehicles: No surge | 2-3 vehicles: 1.2x multiplier | ≤1 vehicle: 1.7x raw (capped to 1.5x)

2. Time Period Surcharge

Morning & Evening Rush Hour (07:00-09:00 / 17:00-19:00): +20% surcharge

Midnight 23:00-06:00: Fixed night service fee

3. Weather / Terminal / Intercity Special Surcharge

Heavy Rain severe weather: Extra +30% markup

Airport & Train Station: Return trip terminal subsidy

Intercity long-distance: Higher base fare, tiered waiting fee

4. Driver Qualification Dispatch Restrictions

✅ Full Vehicle & Driver License: Eligible for all high-surge order pools

❌ Missing one or both licenses: Only short-distance low-fare trips, no high surge access

Service Rating > 95: Priority allocation of high-surge orders; Low rating only budget trips

5. Settlement & Revenue Split Rules

Platform commission cap at 27%, reduced to 25% after monthly order volume target met

Dynamic surcharges and terminal subsidies are excluded from commission calculation base

Highway tolls & parking fees paid separately by passengers, fully transferred to drivers
